Allama Iqbal and his adoration and regard for his teachers

“A teacher is like a tree; it benefits through his shadow in warm grim, fruit whereby we learn, and wood that’s his character to follow.” -Narcissus

𝓝𝓪𝓻𝓬𝓲𝓼𝓼𝓾𝓼 🍃
4 min read
·
Nov 9, 2023

No one can ignore the dignity of a teacher-

MAG
 , what and where we are today, it’s just because of the teacher’s guidance and I think no one can explain the bond of a student and teacher better than Iqbal.

Maulvi Ghulam Hussain:

Allama Iqbal was born on November 9, 1877 on Friday in Sialkot, Punjab, Pakistan. He got underway his studies at the age of five or six. He was fond of the light of knowledge. His father, according to the ritual of that era, got him admitted to the religious school of Maulvi Ghulam Hussain.

Allama Iqbal said about the achievement of knowledge in the school of Maulana Abu Abdul Allah Ghulam Hussain:

"In those days, knowledge and wisdom both had been eliminated from Punjab. My father was eager to make me gain education. First of all, he let me in the Mosque of the street, and then sent me to serve Shah Sahab."

Maulana Mir Hassan:

Maulana Mir Hassan, who became Iqbal's teacher later, often visited Maulana Ghulam Hussain. One day, during his visit, Maulana Mir Hassan saw the little Iqbal there and asked, "Whose child is he?"

Maulana Ghulam replied, "He is the son of Shiekh Noor Muhammed" After that incident, Maulana Mir Hassan met his father and bid,
"Let your child leave the institute of Maulana Ghulam and admit him to mine."

In this way, Iqbal was entrusted to him. Maulana Mir Hassan polished his natural talent and let him introduce not only religious knowledge but also ancient and modern awareness.

I think that's the genuine power of the teacher that he propels the talent of his students to come out...

Allama Iqbal said about teachers,
"Teacher is the Sun."
